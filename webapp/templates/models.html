Reading these results

  • The test split is small ({{ rep.protocol.n_test }} structures) and lumpy: twelve near-identical Li4(Ge,Sn)(S,Se)4 thio- and seleno-germanates/stannates that look fast by geometry but were measured slow sit in it together. One such cluster can swing a 67-row AUC by 0.2, which is why the confidence intervals are wide.
  • Leave-family-out CV uses every labelled structure and holds out whole chemical families, so it is the steadier estimate of performance on genuinely new chemistries.
  • On the independent DFT-MD benchmark the retrained logistic regression orders Sendek's simulated materials best, ahead of the tree models. The tree models fit OBELiX's family structure more closely and transfer worse to new Materials Project chemistry. That is why the logistic regression is the structure ingredient of the CrystalScout Score.
