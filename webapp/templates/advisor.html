How the advisor works

Read the chemistry

Oxidation states make the base neutral ({% for e, q in res.charges.items() if not e.startswith('_') %}{{ e }}{{ '+' if q > 0 }}{{ q }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}).

Generate and filter

Same or neighbouring charge dopants at 10, 25 and 50 %; Li adjusts to stay neutral. Ion-size misfits and non-neutral variants are rejected.

Check existence

Every variant is looked up in Materials Project and OBELiX. Known ones become benchmarks.

Build crystals and score

New variants are built on the base's real crystal (site substitution, Vegard scaling), read back as CIFs and scored with real structure descriptors.

Rank and plan

Plausible candidates rank first; the best comes with a recipe and a CIF.
